About this page

This website describes the Class 10 coaching programme offered by Lakshyha Academy in Bhopal. Batch timings, seat availability and fees shown here are indicative and may change. The details confirmed with you at the centre are the ones that apply.

Demo class

The demo class is free and does not create any obligation on either side. Slots are subject to availability and are confirmed over a phone call.

Fees and refunds

Fees, instalment schedules and the refund position are stated in the admission form you sign at the centre. Registration charges, once paid, are generally non-refundable. Please read the admission form before paying.

Results and claims

Any results, scores or improvements mentioned on this site are past outcomes of individual students. They are not a promise or guarantee of the marks any particular student will score.

Attendance and conduct

Enrolled students are expected to attend regularly, appear for weekly tests, and follow the centre's conduct rules. The academy may discontinue a student's enrolment for repeated absence or misconduct.
