Your Questions, Answered Honestly

Everything You
Need To Know.

Is this freehold or leasehold land? +
100% freehold. You own the land, the structure, and everything on it. Full title deed transfer on possession. This is not apartment-style undivided land share — it is complete, exclusive land ownership.
Can I customise interiors before possession? +
Yes. We offer interior customisation options during the construction window (18 months from booking). Flooring choices, modular kitchen layouts, bathroom fittings, and smart home packages are all customisable within the specification catalogue.
Is home loan available for villas? +
Yes. Pre-approved by SBI, HDFC, and ICICI for up to 75% of the villa value. Our finance desk will run a free eligibility check within 24 hours and guide you through the documentation process.
What is the maintenance structure? +
A dedicated community manager handles all common area maintenance. Individual villa maintenance is the owner's responsibility, but we offer a paid villa maintenance service through our partner vendors. Monthly community maintenance is ₹12,000–₹18,000 depending on villa size.
What is the expected rental yield? +
Premium villas in Kokapet command ₹1.2L–₹2.5L per month in rental for furnished units. This represents a gross yield of 4–5.5% annually, higher than most apartment rentals in the same corridor, with stronger appreciation upside.
Why only 12 villas? Will there be more? +
The 12-villa limit is intentional — it ensures exclusivity of the community, maximises privacy for every villa owner, and keeps the land-to-built ratio premium. There are no plans for expansion phases. Once 12 are sold, the community is permanently closed to new buyers.
