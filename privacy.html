Privacy policy

This page explains what information Lakshyha Academy, Bhopal collects through this website, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you submit the enquiry or demo-booking form, we collect the name, mobile number, email address (if you provide one), board, and area you enter. We also record standard advertising and analytics parameters — such as the Google click identifier and campaign source — so we can tell which advertisement brought you here.

Why we collect it

Solely to contact you about admissions, batch timings, fees and demo classes at Lakshyha Academy, and to measure the performance of our advertising. We do not sell or rent your details to anyone.

How we contact you

By phone call, SMS or WhatsApp on the number you provide, and by email if you gave one. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time and we will remove your details from our follow-up list.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and Google Ads tags. These set cookies that help us understand how visitors use the page and which campaigns produce enquiries. You can block or delete these cookies in your browser settings; the page will still work.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept for as long as the admission conversation is active, and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records. Advertising click data is retained for up to 90 days for attribution.

Children's information

Forms on this site are meant to be filled in by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children.
