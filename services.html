Three things we will never do

We will not add a charge at the registration table that was not in your written agreement. We will not take a commission from the builder and from you on the same deal without telling you. And we will not hold your documents to force a payment.

Government charges are separate and go to the government, not to us: stamp duty, registration fee and applicable GST are payable at actual and shown to you on the receipt.
