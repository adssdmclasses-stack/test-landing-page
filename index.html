Quick answers

Do you charge buyers a fee?

Yes, and you will know the exact amount before we show you anything. It is a percentage of the deal value, agreed in writing at the start. Nothing is added later.

How fast can I see a property?

Usually the same day. Call before 2 pm and we will normally arrange visits for that evening, including pickup.

Can you help if I am not in Hyderabad?

Regularly. We do video walkthroughs, share documents on WhatsApp, and can complete registration with a power of attorney if you cannot travel.

Do you verify legal documents?

Every time. We check the title chain, encumbrance certificate, approvals and RERA registration, and flag anything that looks wrong before money moves.
