How we turned a money-losing ad account into a steady stream of quality calls.

A multi-city roll-off dumpster rental company operating across two US states (Illinois & Wisconsin). Here's what 7 months of focused management looked like.

The Problem

The account was effectively running blind. Conversion tracking was broken, so the bidding algorithm was optimizing toward page views and direction taps instead of real phone calls. Budget was bleeding into competitor searches and off-hours clicks. On paper Google reported a cheap "$103 per conversion" — but the real cost per paying call was over $670, with the account returning just $0.71 for every $1 spent.

What We Did

Rebuilt conversion tracking end-to-end through GTM so the algorithm could finally see real calls, forms, and bookings. Cut junk and competitor spend with aggressive negatives. Restructured into tightly themed, location-specific campaigns across both states and let the algorithm re-learn on clean data.

The Result

Across 7 months, the account generated 727 quality calls (genuine inquiries over 10 seconds) from 820 tracked conversions. Cost per quality call fell about 25% as the rebuilt account stabilized — and for the first time, every dollar of spend was tied to a real, measurable business outcome.
